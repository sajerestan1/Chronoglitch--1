## Scene status
For each planned row, record accepted still, animation/audio asset, generation ID, audio range, duration, preview decision and repair. A scene is complete only after preview and acceptance.

## Recovery notes
Record interruption, last accepted scene, library items checked and next unfinished item. Never record credentials or personal data.
